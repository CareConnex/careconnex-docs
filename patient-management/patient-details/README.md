# Patient Details

### Patient Detail Screen

The **Patient Detail** screen is organized into five tabs: **About, Visit History, Ticket, Credit Wallet, and Plato Medical Record**.

### What You Can Do in Each Tab

Each tab gives you access to different types of patient information and management options.

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image (724).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

#### Tab 1. About

View and update the patient's basic information.

**Key features**

* View personal information such as the patient's name, contact details, and date of birth.
* Edit or delete patient information.

💡 For step-by-step instructions, see the **About** guide.

***

#### Tab 2. Visit History

View the patient's appointments and visit history and manage appointment records.

**Key features**

* View appointment history.
* Edit or delete appointments.
* Check ticket usage and billing details for each appointment.

💡 For step-by-step instructions, see the **Visit History** guide.

***

#### Tab 3. Ticket

View and manage the patient's treatment tickets.

**Key features**

* View available tickets.
* Check ticket usage and remaining sessions linked to appointments.
* Add new tickets.

💡 For step-by-step instructions, see the **Ticket Management** guide.

***

#### Tab 4. Credit Wallet

Manage the patient's Credit Wallet, including credit balances, refunds, and wallet status.

**Key features**

* Add credits.
* Adjust credit balances or issue refunds.
* Change the wallet status.

💡 For step-by-step instructions, see the **Credit Wallet** guide.

***

#### Tab 5. Plato Medical Record

Connect the patient to Plato and view their medical records, appointment history, and billing information.

**Key features**

* Link the patient to their Plato record.
* View medical records.
* View appointment and billing history.

💡 For step-by-step instructions, see the **Plato Medical Record** guide.
