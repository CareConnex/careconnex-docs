# Credit Wallet

{% hint style="info" %}
📘 **In this guide, you'll learn how to:**

* Understand what Credit Wallet is and where to find it
* Check the Credit balance, usage, Wallet status, and expiry date
* Add Credit using **Top Up**
* Adjust or refund Credit
* Manage Wallet status
* Review Wallet activity in **Transaction History**
{% endhint %}

### What Is Credit Wallet?

**Credit Wallet** is a prepaid Credit feature that allows patients to use funds added in advance to pay for treatments and services.

From the patient's **Credit Wallet** tab, you can check the available balance, add or adjust Credit, process refunds, manage Wallet status, and review transaction history.

📌  **Menu path:** Patients > Patient Details > Credit Wallet

## How to Use Credit Wallet

### 1. Check Wallet Information

The **Wallet Summary** shows the patient's current Credit balance, usage, Wallet status, and expiry date.

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image (741).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

<table><thead><tr><th width="192.75">Item</th><th>Description</th></tr></thead><tbody><tr><td><strong>① Current Balance</strong></td><td>Credit currently available for use</td></tr><tr><td><strong>② Total Credited</strong></td><td>Total amount of Credit added to the Wallet</td></tr><tr><td><strong>③ Total Used</strong></td><td>Total amount of Credit used</td></tr><tr><td><strong>④ Status</strong></td><td>Current Wallet status: <strong>ACTIVE / SUSPENDED / CLOSED</strong></td></tr><tr><td><strong>⑤ Expires</strong></td><td>Credit expiry date. If no expiry date is set, the Credit does not expire</td></tr></tbody></table>

> 💡 When the Wallet is **SUSPENDED**, **\[+ Top Up]** and **\[Adjust / Refund]** are disabled.\
> To use these features again, select **\[Manage Status] > \[Reactivate Wallet]** to change the Wallet back to **ACTIVE**.

***

### 2. Add Credit (Top Up)

Use **Top Up** to add Credit to a patient's Wallet. You can also set an expiry date if needed.

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image (742).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

1. In the Wallet Summary, click **\[+ Top Up]**.
2. In the **Top Up Credit** window, enter the following information:
   * **Amount:** Amount of Credit to add **(required)**
   * **Expiry Date:** Credit expiry date **(optional)**
   * **Description:** Note related to the top-up **(optional)**
3. Click **\[Top Up]**.

> 💡 Once the top-up is completed, the updated amount is automatically reflected in **Current Balance** and **Transaction History**.

***

### 3. Adjust or Refund Credit (Adjust / Refund)

Use **Adjust / Refund** to manually correct a patient's Credit balance or process a Credit refund.

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image (12).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

1. In the Wallet Summary, click **\[Adjust / Refund]**.
2. In the **Adjust Credit** window, select a type:
   * **Adjustment:** Manually adjust the Credit amount
   * **Refund:** Refund Credit from the Wallet
3. Enter the following information:
   * **Amount:** Amount to adjust or refund **(required)**
   * **Description:** Note related to the adjustment or refund **(optional)**
4. Click **\[Submit]**.

> 💡 Adjustments and refunds are automatically recorded in **Transaction History**.

***

### 4. Manage Wallet Status

Use **\[Manage Status]** to temporarily suspend or permanently close a patient's Wallet.

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image (744).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

#### ① Suspend Wallet

Use **Suspend Wallet** to temporarily stop the Wallet from being used.

While the Wallet is **SUSPENDED**, the patient cannot use their Credit.

If needed, you can reactivate the Wallet by selecting:

**\[Manage Status] > \[Reactivate Wallet]**

The Wallet will return to **ACTIVE** status.

The following features are unavailable while the Wallet is suspended:

* Adding Credit
* Adjusting or refunding Credit
* Using Credit Wallet as a payment method in Billing

#### ② Close Wallet

Use **Close Wallet** to permanently close the Wallet.

Once the Wallet is closed:

* The Wallet can no longer be used.
* The Wallet cannot be reactivated.
* Any remaining Credit cannot be used for payment.

> ⚠️ A **CLOSED** Wallet cannot be reactivated.\
> Before closing the Wallet, check the remaining Credit balance and complete any necessary adjustments or refunds.

***

### 5. Review Transaction History

**Transaction History** shows all Credit activity in the Wallet, including top-ups, usage, refunds, adjustments, and Wallet status changes.

Transaction types may include:

* **\[Top Up]:** Credit added
* **\[Usage]:** Credit used
* **\[Refund]:** Credit refunded
* **\[Adjustment]:** Credit adjustment or Wallet status change

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image (745).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Each transaction displays the following information:

* **Date:** Date and time of the transaction
* **Type:** Transaction type
* **Amount:** Credit added to or deducted from the Wallet\
  **+** indicates Credit added / **−** indicates Credit deducted
* **Balance:** Credit balance before → after the transaction
* **Description:** Transaction details or notes
* **By:** User who processed the transaction

> 💡 Available Credit can be used as a payment method in **Billing**, and the related activity can be reviewed in **Transaction History**.

***

### 💡 Notes

* To use **Credit Wallet** as a payment method, the patient must have an available Credit balance and the Wallet status must be **ACTIVE**.
* For detailed instructions on using Credit Wallet during payment, see the **Billing & Settlement** guide.

### ❓ Frequently Asked Questions

**Q. Can Credit be used immediately after a top-up?**

Yes. If the Wallet status is **ACTIVE**, the Credit can be used immediately by selecting **Credit Wallet** as the payment method in Billing.

**Q. Do I have to set an expiry date for Credit?**

No. The expiry date is optional. If no expiry date is set, the Credit does not expire.

**Q. What is the difference between Suspend and Close?**

**Suspend** temporarily disables the Wallet and can be reversed using **Reactivate Wallet**.

**Close** permanently closes the Wallet and cannot be reversed.

**Q. Can a closed Wallet be used again?**

No. A **CLOSED** Wallet cannot be reactivated. Check the remaining Credit and complete any necessary adjustments or refunds before closing the Wallet.

**Q. What should I do if I added the wrong amount of Credit?**

Use **\[Adjust / Refund]** to adjust the Credit amount or process a refund.

All changes are automatically recorded in **Transaction History**.
