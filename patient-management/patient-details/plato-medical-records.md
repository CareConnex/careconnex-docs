# Plato Medical Records

{% hint style="info" %}
📘 **In this guide, you'll learn how to:**

* Connect a Dr.OZ patient to a patient in Plato
* View patient information and medical records stored in Plato
* Review clinical notes and attachments
* Check appointment and billing history
* Refresh Plato data to view the latest information
{% endhint %}

## Plato Medical Records Tab

The **Plato Medical Records** tab allows you to view medical records for patients who are connected to Plato directly from Dr.OZ.

📌 **Path: Patients > Patient Details > Plato Medical Records**

⚠️ If Plato has not yet been connected to Dr.OZ, go to **Settings > Hospital Settings > Plato Medical Integration** and complete the integration first. For setup instructions, see the [**Plato Medical Integration**](../../settings/plato-medical-integration.md) guide.

> 💡 **What is Plato?**
>
> Plato is an EMR (Electronic Medical Record) system used to manage patient information and clinical records.
>
> Once Dr.OZ is integrated with Plato, you can access a patient's Plato medical records directly from Dr.OZ.

***

## Connect a Patient to Plato

If the Dr.OZ patient has not yet been connected to Plato, search for the corresponding patient in Plato and link the records.

Once connected, you can review clinical notes, appointment history, billing information, and other Plato data without opening Plato separately.

> 💡 Medical records are not separately stored in Dr.OZ. Dr.OZ retrieves and displays the information stored in Plato.

#### ① Find the Patient in Plato

From the **Plato Medical Records** tab, click **\[Find Plato Patient]**.

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image (747).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

**② Connect the Patient**

Review the search results in the **\[Find Plato Patient]** window.

Check the patient's **name, contact number, date of birth, and other identifying information**. Select the correct patient, then click **\[Connect Selected Patient]**.

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image (748).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

> 💡 **Always verify the patient's information before connecting the records.**
>
> The displayed match level is provided only as a reference to help identify potential matches.

#### ✅ If the Patient Is Not Registered in Plato

If you cannot find the correct patient in the search results, click **\[Register as New Patient in Plato]**.

Before creating a new patient, review the search results once more to make sure the patient does not already exist in Plato.

> 💡 When a new patient is registered, the patient's information is created in Plato and automatically connected to the corresponding patient in Dr.OZ.

#### ③ Confirm the Connection

Click **Connect to Selected Patient** to link the selected Plato patient record to the patient in Dr.OZ.

When the **“Patient linked to Plato”** message appears, the connection is complete. You can then view the patient’s information and medical records in the **Plato Medical Record** tab.

***

## View Plato Medical Records

Once a patient is connected to Plato, you can view patient information and medical records stored in Plato directly from Dr.OZ.



① **Medical Alerts:** View important patient alerts, such as allergies or other information that should be checked before treatment.

② **Plato Patient Information:** View the patient's basic information registered in Plato.

③ **Clinical Notes:** Review clinical records documented in Plato.

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image (750).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

④ **Refresh:** Reload the latest information from Plato.

⑤ **Disconnect:** Disconnect the Dr.OZ patient from the linked Plato patient.

> 💡 Disconnecting a patient only removes the connection between the Dr.OZ patient and the Plato patient. **Patient information and medical records stored in Plato are not deleted.**

***

### Clinical Notes

In **Clinical Notes**, you can review the patient's clinical records stored in Plato.

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image (751).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

> 📁 **View Attachments**
>
> If **\[View Attachment]** appears in a clinical note, click it to open the attached chart image or document in a new window.

***

### Appointment & Billing History

You can review appointments and billing records registered in Plato, with the most recent records shown first.

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image (753).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

> 💡 You can view the appointment date and time together with related **billing amounts and payment information**.

***

## 💡 Notes

* Plato medical records are **view-only** and cannot be edited in Dr.OZ.
* A patient must first be **connected to the corresponding patient in Plato** before their Plato medical records can be viewed in Dr.OZ.
* Medical records are not separately stored in Dr.OZ. Dr.OZ retrieves and displays information stored in Plato.
* If information has been updated in Plato, click **\[Refresh]** to load the latest data.
