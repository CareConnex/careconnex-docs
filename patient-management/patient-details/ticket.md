# Ticket

{% hint style="info" %}
📘 **In this guide, you'll learn how to:**

* View tickets registered to a patient
* Check ticket usage and validity
* Check the next appointment linked to a ticket
* Add a new ticket
{% endhint %}

### View Patient Tickets

📌 📌 **Menu path:** Go to **Patients > Select a patient > Ticket**.

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image (736).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

The Ticket tab displays all tickets registered to the patient, grouped into **Active Tickets** and **Expired Tickets**.

### Active Tickets

Tickets that are currently available for use are displayed as cards.

Each ticket shows key information, including:

* Ticket name and linked service
* Validity period and days remaining
* Used / booked / remaining sessions
* Next appointment date and time
* Assigned provider

> 💡 Click **More >** to view and manage the ticket details.

### Expired Tickets

Tickets that have passed their validity period are listed separately under **Expired Tickets** at the bottom of the page.

### Add a New Ticket

To add a new ticket for the patient, click **\[+ Add Ticket]** in the upper-right corner.

Set the service, number of sessions, validity period, and other required details to issue the ticket to the patient.

> 💡 For complete instructions on using and managing tickets, see the **Ticket Management** guide.
