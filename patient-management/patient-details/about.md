# About

### Patient Information

The **About** tab gives you an overview of the patient's profile information, including contact details, membership status, notes, and other information saved in Dr.OZ.

**📌 Menu path: Patients > Patient Detail > About**

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image (725).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

### Information Available

#### Personal Information

* Name
* Date of Birth
* Gender
* Contact Number
* Resident Registration Number

#### Contact & Membership

* Nationality
* Email
* Membership
* Points

#### Other Information

* Notes
* Referral Source
* App User Status
* Last Visit

💡 To update patient information or delete a patient, see the **Edit / Delete a Patient** guide.
