# Visit History

{% hint style="info" %}
📘 **In this guide, you'll learn how to:**

* Review a patient's appointment and visit history
* Change, edit, or delete an appointment
* Review ticket usage linked to an appointment
* Check invoice details and payment status
{% endhint %}

## Visit History Overview

The **Visit History** tab gives you a complete view of the patient's appointment history and related billing information.

You can review and manage appointment details without switching between the **Schedule** and **Billing** menus.

📌 **Menu path:** **Patients > Patient Details > Visit History**

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image (727).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

#### ① Appointment List

View the patient's appointment and visit history.

The most recent appointments are displayed first.

#### ② Service Tab

View details for the selected appointment, including the service, appointment status, assigned staff, appointment date, and time.

#### ③ History Tab

View ticket usage linked to the selected appointment.

#### ④ Billing Tab

View and manage invoice details and payment status for the selected appointment.

#### ⑤ Filter

Use **Filter** to find appointments based on your preferred criteria.

#### ⑥ More

Use the **More (⋯)** menu to access additional patient management options, such as editing or deleting patient information.

***

## Manage Appointment Details

### Service

In **Visit History > Service**, select an appointment to review its details and manage its status or appointment information.

Use the **Edit (✏️)** or **Delete (🗑️)** icon at the bottom of the appointment details when you need to update or remove the appointment.

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image (728).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

> 💡 **Appointment status availability**
>
> Before the appointment time has passed, the status can be changed to **Confirmed / Completed / Cancelled / No-Show**.
>
> After the appointment time has passed, only **Completed / Cancelled / No-Show** are available.

***

#### Change an Appointment Status

In **Service Appointment**, click the **appointment status** and select the status you want to apply.

The available status options depend on whether the scheduled appointment time has passed.

* **Before the appointment time has passed:** Confirmed / Completed / Cancelled / No-Show
* **After the appointment time has passed:** Completed / Cancelled / No-Show

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image (729).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

> 💡 **When changing an appointment to No-Show**, you can choose to deduct the ticket or update only the appointment status to **No-Show**.

***

#### Edit an Appointment

Use the **Edit (✏️)** icon to update appointment information such as the assigned staff, appointment date and time, or memo.

1. Select the appointment you want to edit.
2. Click the **Edit (✏️)** icon in the Service section.
3. Update the required information and save your changes.

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image (731).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

> 💡 Appointment times can only be selected within the assigned staff member's available working hours.

***

#### Delete an Appointment

You can delete a schedule card from the Service section. Deleting the schedule card also removes the appointment from the schedule and appointment history.

1. Select the appointment you want to delete.
2. Click the **Delete (🗑️)** icon in the Service section.
3. Click **Delete Schedule Card**.
4. In the confirmation window, click **Confirm**.

> ⚠️ Deleted appointments cannot be restored. Check the appointment details carefully before confirming the deletion.

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image (732).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

> 💡 Depending on your browser zoom or screen size, **Delete Schedule Card** may not be immediately visible. Scroll down within the appointment details area if needed.

***

## History

The **History** tab shows ticket usage associated with the selected appointment.

Use this tab to check when a ticket or treatment session was used and review the related service history.

## Billing

In **Visit History > Billing**, you can review and manage invoice and payment information linked to the selected appointment.

For appointments that have not yet reached their scheduled date, billing information may not be available.

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image (734).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

* **Invoice details:** Review the invoice amount, total paid amount, and outstanding balance.
* **Edit billing information:** Click the **Edit (✏️)** icon to update the memo, discount, or tax information.
* **View Invoice:** Open and review the invoice issued for the appointment.
* **Cancel Invoice:** Enter a cancellation reason and cancel the issued invoice.
* **Record Payment:** Record payment details such as the payment date, payment method, and amount.

> 💡 **Payment status**
>
> An invoice with no recorded payment is shown as **Pending**.
>
> Once a payment is recorded, the status changes to **Partially Paid** or **Paid**, depending on the amount received.

### 💡 Notes

* The same invoice and payment management features are also available from the main **Billing** menu.
* For detailed instructions on invoices, payments, cancellations, and payment records, see the **Billing Guide**.
