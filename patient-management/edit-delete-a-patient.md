# Edit / Delete a Patient

### Edit Patient Information

📌 **Menu path:** Patients > Select a patient > **More** > **Edit Patient Information**

#### 1. Open the Patient Profile

Click the patient's **name** in the patient list to open their profile.

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/image (717).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

#### 2. Open Edit Patient Information

From the patient profile, click **More > Edit Patient Information**.

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/image (718).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

#### 3. Update Patient Information

In the **Edit Patient** window, update the information as needed.

> ⚠️ You cannot save the changes if any required fields are left blank.

#### 4. Save Your Changes

Click **Confirm** to save the updated information.

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/image (719).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

#### 5. Confirm the Update

Once the changes are saved, a **“Patient information has been updated.”** message will appear.

***

### Delete a Patient

**Menu path:** Patients > Select a patient > **More** > **Delete Patient**

#### 1. Select a Patient

From the patient list, click the **name** of the patient you want to delete to open their profile.

#### 2. Delete the Patient

From the patient profile, click **More > Delete Patient** to proceed.

> ⚠️ Deleting a patient may take effect immediately. Please make sure you have selected the correct patient before proceeding.

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/image (720).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

#### 3. Confirm the Deletion

When the deletion confirmation message appears, click **Confirm**.

The patient list will refresh and return you to the Patients page.

#### 4. Force Delete

If the patient has existing **consultation or treatment history**, standard deletion may be restricted.

In this case, you can use **Force Delete** to remove the patient.

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/image (721).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

> ⚠️ **Use Force Delete with caution.**\
> Deleted patient information may be difficult to recover. Make sure you have reviewed any information you may need before proceeding.

***

### 📌 Important Notes

* Required fields cannot be left blank when editing patient information.
* Patients with existing consultation or treatment history may not be eligible for standard deletion.
* Information removed through **Force Delete** may be difficult to recover. Review the patient's information carefully before deleting.

***

### ❓ Frequently Asked Questions

**Q. Why can't I delete a patient?**\
Patients with existing consultation or treatment history may be restricted from standard deletion. In this case, you can use **Force Delete** to remove the patient.
