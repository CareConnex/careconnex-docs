# Patient Registration

### Patient Registration & Search

#### Register a New Patient

1. Go to **Patients** and click **\[Add Patient]**.
2. Enter the patient's information in the **Add Patient** window.
3. Complete all required fields, then click **\[Add]** to finish registration.

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/image (713).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

> ✅ **Required fields**
>
> * Name
> * Date of Birth
> * Gender
> * Mobile Number
> * Nationality
> * Membership

* **Optional fields:** Notes, Referral Source, Email, Points

> ⚠️ Fields marked with an `*` are required. All required fields must be completed before the patient can be registered.

***

### Search for a Patient

Use the **search bar** or **filters** at the top left of the Patients page to quickly find a patient.

Enter a keyword or apply filter criteria to display patients that match your search.

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/image (714).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

#### ① **Search by Keyword**

You can search for patients using information such as:

* Name
* Phone number
* Notes
* Patient ID

You can also search using only part of a Patient ID.

For example:

`DEMO-020` → Search for `26071421`

#### ② **Search with Filters**

Use filters to narrow down the patient list by criteria such as **Date of Birth, Gender, or Membership**.

Click **\[Filter]** → select the desired criteria in **Search Filters** → click **\[Confirm]** to apply the filters.

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/image (715).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

> 💡 **Tip**
>
> To clear the applied filters, click the **Refresh (🔄) icon** next to the Filter button.

***

### ❓ Frequently Asked Questions

**Q. Can patients see the membership tier selected during registration?**\
Yes. The membership tier assigned to the patient is also displayed in the Dr.OZ patient app under **My Page > My Clinic**.

**Q. Can I register multiple patients at once?**\
Yes. If you need to register patients in bulk, prepare a patient information file containing all required fields and send it to your **Dr.OZ support contact**. We will assist you with the bulk registration process.
