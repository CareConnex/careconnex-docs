# Clinic Information Setup

## Set Up Your Clinic Information

> Add the basic information Dr.OZ needs to set up your clinic.\
> Prepare the details below and provide them to your Dr.OZ representative when requested.

### 📝 Basic Information

Please prepare the following information:

* Clinic Name
* Clinic ID (URL)
  * ✅ Your Clinic ID is used to create your clinic's unique Dr.OZ access URL.
  * Example: `https://your-clinic-id.careconnex.io`
* Clinic Representative
* Contact Number
* Address
* Administrator Email
* Business Hours
* Clinic Logo (Optional)

***

### 📌 Notes

* Your **clinic name, contact number, and address** may appear in patient-facing messages, reminders, receipts, and other communications.
* Use an email address that will be used for the clinic administrator account. **Gmail is recommended.**
* Most clinic information can be updated later from **Settings**. If you need help changing information or cannot edit a specific field, contact your Dr.OZ representative. ✉️ `official@careconnex.io`

***

### ❓ Frequently Asked Questions

**Q. Can I add more staff accounts later?**

Yes. You can add staff members anytime from the **Members** menu.

For detailed instructions, see the **Member Management** guide.

> An email address is required for each staff account.

**Q. Can I update my clinic information later?**

Yes. You can update your clinic information in: **Settings > Clinic Settings > Clinic Information**

Business hours can be managed separately under: **Settings > Clinic Settings > Business Hours**
