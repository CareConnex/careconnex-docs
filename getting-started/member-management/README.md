# Member Management

## Add & Manage Members

> Add your clinic staff to Dr.OZ and manage their account information, permissions, and login access.

### 1. Add a Member

① Go to **Members** from the left navigation bar.

② Click **\[+ Add Member]**.

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image (689).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

③ Enter the staff member's information and complete all required fields.

> 📌 **Required:** Name, Email (Login ID), Date of Birth, Start Date

④ Click **\[Add]** to create the member account.

#### ⑤ Check the Invitation Email & Sign In

Once the account is created, an invitation email will be sent to the member's registered email address.

The email contains a temporary password for their first login.

* Click **\[Start Dr.OZ]** in the invitation email to open your clinic's Dr.OZ page.

<div><figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image (118).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure> <figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/이메일 편집본.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure></div>

> ⚠️ For security, change the temporary password after the first login.
>
> To change the password, click the **profile image at the bottom of the left navigation bar > \[My Profile]**.

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image (691).png" alt="" width="375"><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

### 2. Manage Account Permissions

New accounts are assigned the **Member** role by default.

Available menus and features vary depending on the assigned role, so the Dr.OZ interface may look different for each user.

If a staff member needs additional access, select **Manager** or **Administrator** under **Account Permissions** when adding the member.

* Access levels increase in the following order:\
  **Member → Manager → Administrator**

### 3. Manage Member Information

Select a member and open the **More \[...]** menu in the upper-right corner to manage their account.

From this menu, you can:

* Edit the member's profile
* Reset their password
* Delete the member account

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image (695).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

#### ① Edit a Member Profile

Select the member, then go to:

**More \[...] > \[Edit Member Profile]**

You can update the member's profile information and working hours.

#### ② Reset a Password

If a member cannot access their account, an Administrator can generate a new temporary password.

1. Select the member.
2. Go to **More \[...] > \[Reset Password]**.
3. Click **\[Reset Password]** to generate a new temporary password.

> ⚠️ The temporary password is displayed **only once**.
>
> Copy it before closing the window and share it securely with the staff member.

#### ③ Delete a Member

To remove a staff account, select the member and go to:

**More \[...] > \[Delete Member]**

> ⚠️ Deleting a member will also delete schedules associated with that member's profile.
>
> **Deleted data cannot be restored.** Please check carefully before deleting the account.

### ❓ Frequently Asked Questions

**Q. How can I add multiple staff members at once?**

If you need to add multiple staff members, please contact your Dr.OZ representative.

Provide the following information for each staff member:

* Name
* Login ID (Email)
* Date of Birth
* Start Date

✉️ `official@careconnex.io`

**Q. What if a staff member forgets their password?**

They can use **\[Forgot Password]** on the login page.

Alternatively, an Administrator can generate a new temporary password from:

**Members > \[...] > \[Reset Password]**

**Q. Will the new temporary password be sent by email automatically?**

No. A temporary password generated through **\[Reset Password]** is **not sent by email automatically**.

Copy the generated password and share it directly with the staff member.
