# Department Settings

## Add & Manage Departments

> Create departments based on your clinic's organizational structure and assign staff members to the appropriate department for easier management.

**Examples:**

* Medical Team
* Consultation Team
* Front Desk
* Administration Team

### 1. Add a Department

1. Go to **Members** from the left navigation bar.
2. Click ⚙ **\[Department Settings]** below the **\[+ Add Member]** button.
3. In the **\[Department Settings]** window, click **\[+ Add Department]**.
4. Enter the department name and click **\[Save]**.

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image (696).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

> 📌 Once a department is created, it can be selected as a staff member's department when adding or editing a member.

***

### 2. Manage Departments

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image (697).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

**Edit a Department**

Open ⚙ **\[Department Settings]**, then click the **Edit (✏️) icon** next to the department you want to update.

Edit the department name and save your changes.

**Delete a Department**

Click the **Delete (🗑️) icon** next to the department you want to remove.

**Change the Display Order**

Drag the **reorder icon (≡)** to the left of a department name to change the order in which departments are displayed.

**Change a Department Color**

Click the **color indicator** for a department and select a new color.

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image (700).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

> Colors that are already assigned to another department cannot be selected.

***

### ❓ Frequently Asked Questions

**Q. Is there a limit to the number of departments I can create?**

No. You can create as many departments as needed for your clinic.
