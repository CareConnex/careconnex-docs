# Promotion Management

{% hint style="info" %}
📘 **In this guide, you'll learn how to:**

* Create a promotion and set its display period
* Configure pricing, discounts, and ticket options
* Check how promotions appear in the patient app and how patients can make inquiries
* View and continue promotion inquiries in Inbox
{% endhint %}

## Create a Promotion

① Go to **Promotions** and click **\[+ Add Promotion]** in the upper-right corner.

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/image (17).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

**② On the Add New Promotion page, enter the promotion details in each section below.**<br>

***

### 1. Basic Information

Enter the basic information patients will see in the app.

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/image (18).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

**Category**

Select the category where the promotion should appear.

**Promotion Title**

Enter the promotion name displayed in the patient app.

> Up to 50 characters can be entered.

**Description**

Add a short description of the promotion.

The description will appear with the promotion in the patient app.

> Up to 100 characters can be entered.

***

### 2. Add Images

Upload a **thumbnail image** and a **detail image** for the promotion.

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/image (19).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

**Thumbnail**

The thumbnail is the main image shown in the promotion list.

* **Recommended size:** 310 × 140 px
* **Supported formats:** JPG / JPEG / PNG

**Detail Image**

This image is displayed when a patient opens the promotion detail page.

* **Recommended size:** 310 × 640 px
* **Supported formats:** JPG / JPEG / PNG

> 💡 If the image is taller than 640 px, patients can select **\[View More]** in the app to view the full image.

***

### 3. Set the Display Period

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/image (21).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Choose when the promotion should appear in the patient app.

**Always**

The promotion remains available without a set end date.

**Set Period**

Choose a start date and end date.

The promotion will only be displayed during the selected period.

***

### 4. Payment & Promotion Item Settings

Choose whether to enable payment and add the product or ticket being offered as part of the promotion.

#### Payment

* **ON:** The **Promotion Item** section becomes available, allowing you to set pricing, discounts, and ticket details.
* **OFF:** The **Promotion Item** section is disabled.

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/image (24).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

> 💡 Promotion items can only be added when **Payment is ON**.\
> Patients can still use **1:1 Inquiry** even when Payment is turned OFF.

#### Promotion Item

**① Ticket Name**

Enter the name of the treatment, package, or promotion item.

Examples: Pico Toning, Rejuran

**② Selling Price**

Enter the selling price of the promotion item.

The price will be displayed using the currency configured for your clinic.

**③ Instant Discount**

Choose whether to apply an instant discount.

When enabled, select either:

* **Discount Rate (%)**, or
* **Discount Amount**

The discounted price will be calculated based on your selection.

**④ Ticket (Sessions)**

Enter the number of sessions included in the ticket.

Example: 1–100 sessions

**⑤ Ticket Validity**

Choose how long the ticket can be used.

Available options include:

* 365 days
* 180 days
* 90 days
* 30 days
* No expiration
* Custom

If you select **Custom**, set the ticket's start and end dates.

> 💡 If the promotion's **Display Period** is set to **Always**, the ticket validity must also be set to **No expiration**.

**⑥ Add Multiple Items**

Click the **\[+]** button below the Promotion Item section to add multiple tickets or packages to the same promotion.

***

### 5. Ticket Usage Information & Promotion Notes

Add any information patients should know before purchasing or using the promotion.

**Ticket Usage Information**

Enter instructions on how the ticket can be used.

Examples:

* Please present your ticket when visiting the clinic.
* Advance booking is required.

**Promotion Notes**

Add any important conditions, precautions, or treatment-related information.

Examples:

* Pregnant patients should consult the clinic before treatment.
* Patients with sensitive skin are advised to consult the medical team before proceeding.

***

### 6. Preview & Save

#### Preview the Promotion

Before saving, click **\[Preview]** to check how the promotion will appear in the patient app.

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/image (25).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

You can preview two screens:

* **Thumbnail**
* **Detail Page**

> 💡 Preview lets you check how the thumbnail and detail images you uploaded will appear to patients in the app.

#### Save

Once all information has been entered, click **\[Save]** to register the promotion.

* Saved promotions are displayed in the patient app according to their status and display period.

***

## Patient App

Patients can view registered promotions directly in the Dr.OZ app.

The promotion detail page may include:

* Promotion image
* Discounted price
* Regular price
* Promotion details

#### **Receive Promotion Inquiries from Patients**

Patients can view a promotion in the Dr.OZ app and send an inquiry directly to the clinic.

① Select a **promotion** from the home screen.\
② On the promotion detail page, select the **price banner** to activate the **\[1:1 Inquiry]** button.\
③ Tap **\[1:1 Inquiry]** to open Inquiry. A message containing the selected promotion information is automatically created.\
④ Once the patient sends the message, clinic staff can immediately identify which promotion the inquiry is about and continue the conversation in Inbox.

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/image (26).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

***

### 💡 View Promotions from Another Clinic

The patient app shows promotions for the **currently selected clinic**.

To view promotions from another clinic, select the **clinic name ▾** in the upper-left corner of the app and choose a different clinic.

* If the clinic is in another country, select **Country/Region** and choose the desired country.
* Select the clinic you want to view.
* Tap **\[Save]** to apply the change.
* Promotions registered for the newly selected clinic will then be displayed.

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/타지점 프로모션 (1).png" alt="" width="563"><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

***

## Inbox Integration

Promotion inquiries sent by patients are received in:

**Inquiries > Inbox**

Clinic staff can open the conversation, check which promotion the patient is referring to, and continue the consultation directly from Inbox.

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/image (712).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

***

## 💡 Notes

* Changes made to an existing promotion are also reflected in the patient app.
* Deactivated promotions are no longer displayed in the patient app.
* For more information about using the patient app, refer to the [**Patient App Guide**.](https://app.gitbook.com/s/bQTCYuRXYU3K1UkronJM/mobile-app)
