# Ticket Management

{% hint style="info" %}
📘 **In this guide, you'll learn how to:**

* Issue a new ticket to a patient
* Create an appointment using an issued ticket
* Check available and expired tickets
{% endhint %}

### What Is a Ticket?

A **ticket** allows a patient to use a specific service or treatment for a set number of sessions.

When a ticket is linked to an appointment, Dr.OZ tracks the **sessions used and sessions remaining**. A ticket is shown as expired once all sessions have been used or its validity period has ended.

📌 **Path:** Patient > Select a patient > **Tickets**

***

### Issue a Ticket

You can issue a ticket to a patient before creating a treatment appointment.

#### ① Open the patient's Tickets tab

Select a patient and go to **Tickets**, then click **\[+ Add Ticket]** on the right.

#### ② Add a service

When the **Add Ticket** window opens, go to the **Service** tab and click **\[+ Add Service]**.

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/image (825).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

#### ③ Select a service

Select the service you want to include in the ticket, then click **\[Confirm]**.

* Select the checkbox next to a service to review your selections in the **Selected Services** panel on the right.

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/image (827).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

> 💡 Check the **service name, treatment option, number of sessions, and selling price** before making your selection.

#### ④ Review and configure the ticket

In the **Add Ticket** window, review and enter the ticket information, then click **\[Add]**.

> 💡 Check the **number of sessions, validity period, payment price, and payment type (Pre-paid / Post-paid)** before issuing the ticket.
>
> * If the ticket does not have an expiry date, select **No Expiry**.

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/image (828).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

> 💡 Click **\[+ Add Service]** to add multiple services to a single ticket. When creating an appointment, you can select which service from the ticket will be performed.

#### ⑤ Complete ticket issuance

In the **Ticket Added** window, click **\[Confirm]** to complete the ticket issuance.

* Select **\[Issue Another Ticket]** if you want to add another ticket for the patient.

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/image (829).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

***

### Book an Appointment with a Ticket

Once a ticket has been issued, you can use it when creating an appointment for the patient.

#### ① Click \[Book Now]

Open the issued ticket and click **\[Book Now]**.

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/image (833).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

#### ② Enter the appointment details

In the **Book Patient** window, set the appointment date and time, select the service to be performed, assign a staff member, and complete any other required information.

Then click **\[Confirm]**.

Once the appointment is created successfully, a confirmation message will appear.

* You can view the registered appointment from the **Schedule** menu.

***

#### Manage Tickets

Go to **Patient > Tickets** to view and manage the patient's tickets.

Tickets are grouped by status:

* **Available Tickets:** Tickets that can currently be used
* **Expired Tickets:** Tickets whose sessions have all been used or whose validity period has ended

Select a ticket under **Available Tickets** to view more information from the **Service / History** tabs.

* **Service tab:** Check ticket details such as the validity period and remaining sessions.
* **History tab:** Review the ticket's usage history.

Click **More (⋮)** on a ticket to access the following options:

**Change Validity Period, Deduct Sessions, Delete, and Refund.**

***

#### Change the Validity Period or Deduct Sessions

You can update the validity period or manually deduct sessions from an issued ticket.

① Open the ticket and click **More (⋮)**, then select the option you need:

* **Change Validity Period:** Select a new expiry date. The remaining days will be calculated automatically.
* **Deduct Sessions:** Select the number of sessions to deduct without creating an appointment.

② Review the changes, then click **\[Save]**.

> 💡 You can deduct up to **4 sessions at a time**.

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/image (835).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

***

#### Delete a Ticket

① Open the ticket and click **More (⋮) > \[Delete]**.

② Review the **Ticket Deletion Notes**, enter a reason for deletion, and click **\[Save]**.

③ In the confirmation window, click **\[Confirm]** to delete the ticket.

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/image (836).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

> ⚠️ **Check before deleting a ticket**\
> Deleting a ticket also deletes its related reservations and procedure records, and any existing appointments will be canceled automatically. **Deleted tickets cannot be restored.**\
> If the ticket has already been paid for, process the refund separately if required.

***

#### Refund a Ticket

You can refund the **unused sessions of a Pre-paid ticket**.

① Go to **More (⋮) > \[Refund]** and review the number of sessions used and unused, as well as the **Net Refund** amount.

② Select the refund method and, if needed, enter a refund reason. Then click **\[Confirm Refund]**.

> 💡 The refund amount is calculated based on the **unused sessions**. If an **Admin Fee** applies, it is deducted to calculate the final **Net Refund**.

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/image (838).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

> ⚠️ **Post-paid tickets are not eligible for Refund because no payment is collected in advance.**\
> To cancel a session that has already been paid for, process the cancellation from the patient's payment history.
