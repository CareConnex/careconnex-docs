# Inbox (Home)

{% hint style="info" %}
📘 **In this guide, you'll learn how to:**

* Review and respond to customer inquiries
* Link an existing patient or register a new patient
* Write and send messages and check recent reminders
* Manage and close inquiries
* Review previous conversation history and follow messaging guidelines
{% endhint %}

### Inquiry Inbox

The **Inquiry Inbox** brings customer inquiries from multiple channels into one place, making it easy to review and manage conversations.

📌 **Path: Dr.OZ > Inquiries > Inbox**

***

### Inbox Layout

The Inbox is divided into three main areas.

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/image (778).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

* **Left — Inquiry List:** Select and manage customer inquiries.
* **Center — Chat Window:** Review messages and continue the conversation.
* **Right — Information Panel:** View and manage inquiry details, patient information, and available AI tools.

> 💡 **Tip:** In the **Inquiry List** on the left, you can see which social media channel each customer used to contact your clinic. If your clinic manages multiple social media accounts, use the channel information to quickly identify where each inquiry came from.

***

### Conversation Workflow

Following the workflow below can help your team manage customer inquiries more efficiently.

#### 💬 During the Conversation

**① Check the inquiry → ② Review and link patient information → ③ Reply to the customer and use available AI tools**

* Review the customer's inquiry and previous messages.
* Link the customer to a patient profile when applicable.
* Respond using a manually written message, message template, or available AI-assisted reply features.

#### ✅ Follow-up

**④ Create, update, or delete an appointment → ⑤ Manage the conversation → ⑥ Close the inquiry**

* Complete any required appointment-related tasks.
* Review the assignee, labels, groups, and conversation notes as needed.
* Once all required follow-up is complete, change the inquiry status to **Closed**.

***

### 1. Check Inquiries

Customer inquiries from connected channels are collected under **Inquiries > Inbox** and organized by assignment and conversation status.

#### ① Check Unassigned Inquiries

Select the **\[Unassigned]** tab in the inquiry list to view new inquiries that have not yet been assigned to a staff member.

* Before the conversation begins, the assignee is displayed as **Unassigned** in the inquiry information panel.

#### ② Start the Conversation and Assign an Owner

When a staff member sends the first message, that staff member is automatically assigned to the inquiry, and the conversation moves to the **\[Assigned]** tab.

* The assigned staff member's name is automatically displayed in the inquiry information panel on the right.

#### ③ Check Closed Inquiries

Completed inquiries are moved to the **\[Closed]** tab.

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/image (779).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

#### 💡 Tips

* When a new message arrives, the inquiry is visually indicated in the inquiry list.
* Turn on the **\[Unread]** filter to quickly find unread inquiries.
* Use **Search / Filter / Sort** to find the conversation you need more quickly.

***

### 2. Link Patient Information

Before replying, check the **\[Patient]** tab on the right to see whether the inquiry is already linked to an existing patient.

* If a suggested patient appears, review the information and link the correct patient.
* If no patient is linked, use **\[Add Patient]** to search for an existing patient.
* For a new customer, select **\[Register Patient]** to create a new patient profile.

> 📌 Once a patient is linked, you can review patient information together with previous conversation history.
>
> For detailed instructions, see the **Patient Linking** guide.

***

### 3. Reply to Inquiries

Use the chat window in the center of the screen to review the customer's messages and send your reply.

#### ① Review the Conversation

Select a customer from the inquiry list on the left.

The conversation with that customer will appear in the center chat window.

#### ② Check Recent Reminders

If a customer replies after receiving a reminder message, the inquiry will appear in **Inbox (Home)**.

When a recent reminder is available, **\[View reminders sent since the last consultation/inquiry]** appears at the top of the chat. This allows you to check the latest reminder sent to the customer.

Select **\[View]** to open the **Reminder Delivery Log**, where you can check:

* The reminder message that was sent
* Date and time sent
* Delivery success or failure

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/image (780).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

> 💡 If a customer replies after receiving a reminder, check which reminder was sent before responding. This gives you useful context and helps you continue the conversation more naturally.

> 📌 For reminder settings and complete delivery history, see the [**Reminder Management** ](https://app.gitbook.com/s/bQTCYuRXYU3K1UkronJM/reminder-management)guide.

#### ③ Write and Send a Message

Enter your reply in the message field at the bottom of the chat window.

Press **Enter** or select the **Send (➜)** button to send the message to the customer.

> 💡 To add a line break without sending the message, press **Shift + Enter**.

***

### 4. Manage Conversations

#### Check the Assignee

When a staff member sends the first message on an unassigned inquiry, that staff member is automatically assigned to the inquiry.

For additional assignment and conversation management options, see the **Conversation Management** guide.

#### Manage Unread Status

If an inquiry requires additional review or follow-up, turn on the **\[Unread]** switch at the top of the chat.

You can later find the inquiry again using the **\[Unread]** filter in the inquiry list.

> 💡 Unread status is used internally by staff and is not visible to the customer.

#### ⚠️ Messaging Guidelines

Messages are sent through the same channel the customer used to contact your clinic.

**Sent messages cannot be edited or recalled, so always review the message before sending.**

Depending on the messaging channel's policy, standard messages may be restricted after a certain period has passed since the customer's last message.

If a message cannot be sent, select **\[View Reason]** to check the cause.

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/image (781).png" alt="" width="512"><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

✅ **Common delivery restrictions include:**

* Facebook, Instagram, and WhatsApp may restrict standard messages after the permitted messaging window has expired.
* Depending on the channel, you may need to use an **approved message template** or wait until the customer sends a new message before replying. (Note: [Meta-guide](https://developers.facebook.com/documentation/business-messaging/whatsapp/support/error-codes))

> 📌 For instructions on registering and using approved message templates, see the [**Message Template Management**](message-template-management.md) guide.

***

### 5. Close an Inquiry

Once the customer's questions have been answered and all required follow-up tasks are complete, close the inquiry.

#### ① Before Closing an Inquiry

Before closing an inquiry, check that:

* The customer's questions have been fully answered.
* Any required appointment creation, changes, or cancellations have been completed.
* The patient has been linked or registered when necessary.
* There are no remaining follow-up items.
* Any necessary conversation notes have been saved.
* Required assignees, labels, and groups have been set correctly.

#### ② Close the Inquiry

When the conversation is complete, select **Inquiry Status** at the top of the chat and change the status to **\[Closed]**.

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/image (782).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

> 💡 If follow-up is still required, do not close the inquiry yet. Use **Unread** status or conversation notes to keep track of it.

***

### Send Messages via the Dr.OZ App

If there is no existing inquiry and your clinic needs to contact a patient first, use **\[+ New Chat]**.

> 💡 A new chat can only be started with patients who have completed **My Clinic verification** in the Dr.OZ app.
>
> Patients who previously completed verification but have since deleted their account may not appear in search results.

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/image (783).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

#### ① Open a New Chat

Select **\[+ New Chat]** from the Inbox.

#### ② Search for and Select a Patient

In the **New Chat** pop-up, search for the patient and select **\[Start Chat]** next to the patient you want to contact.

#### ③ Start the Conversation

Select **\[Start Chat]** to open the patient's chat and begin the conversation.

> 💡 If the patient already has an active conversation, Dr.OZ will not create a duplicate chat. You will be redirected to the existing conversation instead.

### View Conversation History

For inquiries linked to a patient, you can review previously closed conversations from **\[Conversation History]** in the **Patient** tab on the right.

📌 **Path: Inbox chat > Patient tab > Conversation History > Select a closed conversation > \[Go to Inquiry]**

* Select a closed conversation to preview previous messages in the **Conversation History** window.
* Select **\[Go to Inquiry]** to open the full conversation.

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/image (785).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

> 💡 Use conversation history to review a returning patient's previous requests and conversations before responding.
