# Patient Linking

{% hint style="info" %}
📘 **In this guide, you'll learn how to:**

* Find and link an existing patient
* Review linked patient information and unlink a patient
* Search for additional existing patients
* Register a new patient
{% endhint %}

***

### Link Patient Information

Linking an Inbox inquiry to an existing patient profile in Dr.OZ allows you to review the patient's information and previous conversation history while responding.

Before replying to the customer, check the **\[Patient]** tab on the right side of the chat to see whether an existing patient profile is available.

📌 **Path: Inquiries > Inbox > \[Patient] tab > \[Info] tab**

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/image (790).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

***

### 1. Find and Link a Patient

Dr.OZ uses the customer's phone number or connected channel account information to identify **possible matches with existing patient profiles**.

Patient profiles are not linked automatically. Review the suggested patient information first, then select **\[Link]** to connect the correct patient.

If the patient you are looking for does not appear in the suggested results, use **\[Search More Patients]** to search existing patient records manually.

#### ① Review the Patient Match and Link

Review the patient's details, such as their **name and phone number**, then select **\[Link]** for the correct patient.

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/image (803).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

> ⚠️ Patients may have the same or similar names. Before linking a patient, confirm additional information such as the **phone number or date of birth** to make sure you select the correct profile.

#### ② Review Linked Patient Information

Once the patient is successfully linked:

* A confirmation message appears at the top of the screen.
* The linked patient's information is displayed in the **\[Patient]** tab on the right.

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/image (802).png" alt="" width="563"><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

> 💡 Linking a patient gives you access to their basic information and previous conversation history. You can also handle appointment-related tasks, such as creating, updating, or cancelling appointments, while assisting the customer.

***

### 2. Search for an Existing Patient

If the patient you need does not appear in the suggested matches, select **\[Search More Patients]**.

From the Patient menu, search by **name, phone number, or patient number** to check whether the customer already has an existing patient profile.

### Register a New Patient

If you still cannot find an existing patient after using **\[Search More Patients]**, select **\[Register Patient]** from the **\[Info]** tab to create a new patient profile.

> ⚠️ To prevent duplicate patient records, search by the customer's **name and phone number** before creating a new profile.

***

### Unlink a Patient

If the wrong patient has been linked, select **\[Unlink]** under **Linked Patient Information**, then select **\[Unlink]** again in the confirmation window.

After unlinking the patient, search for the correct patient and link the appropriate profile.

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/image (791).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>



***

### 📌 Tips

* Link the patient before starting the conversation whenever possible. This allows you to review previous inquiries and patient information in advance, helping you respond **more quickly and accurately**.
* For detailed instructions on creating a new patient profile, see the **Patient Registration** guide.
