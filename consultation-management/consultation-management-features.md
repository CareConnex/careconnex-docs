# Consultation Management Features

{% hint style="info" %}
📘 **In this guide, you'll learn how to:**

* Check how inquiry assignees are assigned
* Create and manage labels
* Create and manage groups
* Search inquiries using labels and groups
* Add and Manage Inquiry Memos
{% endhint %}



## Manage Customer Inquiries

Use the Inbox information panel to check the assignee, organize inquiries with labels and groups, and leave internal notes for your team.

📌 **Path: Inquiries > Inbox > Select an inquiry > \[Inquiry] or \[Patient] tab in the right information panel**

***

### Manage Assignees

New inquiries do not have an assignee by default. When a staff member sends the **first reply**, that staff member is automatically assigned to the inquiry.

* Once assigned, the inquiry moves from the **\[Unassigned]** tab to the **\[Assigned]** tab in the inquiry list.
* You can check the current assignee under **\[Inquiry] > \[Assignee]** in the right information panel.

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/image (792).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

**✅ Assignee Status**

* **Unassigned:** No staff member has been assigned yet.
* **Assignee name displayed:** The staff member who sent the first reply has been assigned to the inquiry.

## Manage Labels

Labels help your team categorize inquiries based on the type or status of a conversation.

Examples include:

* New Customer
* VIP
* Appointment Inquiry
* Treatment Inquiry
* Spam

Multiple labels can be applied to the same inquiry.

***

### 1. Manage Labels

#### Create a New Label

① In the **\[Inquiry]** tab, click the **Add Label (+)** button.

② In the **Label Settings** window, click **\[+ Add New Label]** and enter a label name.

③ Click **\[Save]** to create the label.

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/image (793).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

***

#### Edit a Label

① In the **\[Inquiry]** tab, click the **Add Label (+)** button.

② In **Label Settings**, click the **Edit (✏️)** icon next to the label you want to update.

> 💡 After selecting **\[+ Add New Label]**, click the **Trash (🗑️)** icon to cancel the new label entry before saving.

***

#### Apply Labels

① Select the inquiry you want to organize from the inquiry list.

② In the right-side **\[Inquiry]** tab, click **Add Label (+)**.

③ In **Label Settings**, select the labels you want to apply.

> 💡 You can select multiple labels. The number of selected labels is displayed next to **Label** in the Label Settings window.

***

#### Remove a Label

If a label was applied by mistake, go to **\[Inquiry] > \[Label]** and click the **X** next to the label you want to remove.

Removing a label from an inquiry **does not delete the label itself**.

> 💡 **Tip:** Hover over the label icon in the inquiry list to preview the labels applied to an inquiry without opening the conversation.

***

### 2. Manage Groups

Groups help organize inquiries based on your clinic's teams or operational structure.

Examples:

* Call Center
* Coordinator Team
* Nursing Team
* Marketing Team

> 💡 Registered groups can also be used when searching and filtering inquiries. Create groups based on the teams or workflows your clinic commonly uses.

***

#### Add a Group

① Go to **\[Patient] > \[Group]** and click the **Add Group (+)** button.

② In **Group Settings**, select an existing group or create a new one.

③ Click **\[Save]** to apply the group to the patient.

> 💡 To remove an applied group, click the **X** next to the group name. Removing the group from a patient does **not delete the registered group itself**.

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/image (794).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

***

#### Edit a Group

① Go to **\[Patient] > \[Group]** and click the **Add Group (+)** button.

② In **Group Settings**, click the **Edit (✏️)** icon next to the group name you want to update.

③ Make your changes and click **\[Save]**.

***

### 3. Search by Group or Label

Use the **Filter** at the top of the inquiry list to quickly find inquiries based on registered groups or labels.

#### Search by Group

① Click the **Filter (funnel)** icon at the top of the inquiry list.

② Select **\[Group]**.

③ Select one or more groups, then click **\[Confirm]**.

④ Only inquiries matching the selected conditions will be displayed.

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/image (796).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

> 💡 **Reset Filters:** Click the **Reset (↻)** icon at the top of the Search Filter window to clear all currently applied filter conditions.

***

#### Search by Label

As with group searches, open the Search Filter, select **\[Label]**, choose the labels you want to use, and click **\[Confirm]**.

**\[Insert image: Searching inquiries by Label]**

> 💡 You can select multiple groups and labels and apply both types of filters together to narrow down your inquiry search.

***

## Manage Inquiry Memos

Use **\[Inquiry] > \[Inquiry Memo]** in the right information panel to record internal notes that should be shared only with your team.

Examples:

* Prefers weekend appointments
* Follow-up call required
* Doctor confirmation needed
* Interested in a specific treatment

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/image (801).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

***

### 1. Add a Consultation Note

Enter your note under **\[Inquiry] > \[Inquiry Memo]**, then click **\[Save]**.

* Each saved note shows the **date/time and author**.
*   The **most recent note appears at the top**

    #### 1. Add an Inquiry Memo

    Enter your note in the **\[Inquiry Memo]** field, then click **\[Save]**.

    * Each saved memo shows the **date/time and author**.
    * The **most recent memo appears at the top**.

***

### 2. Edit or Delete a Note

Saved consultation notes can be edited or deleted at any time.

* **Edit:** Click **More (⋮) > \[Edit]**
* **Delete:** Click **More (⋮) > \[Delete]**, then click **\[Confirm]** in the confirmation window.

## ❓ Frequently Asked Questions

**Q. Can I manually change the assignee?**

A. Manual reassignment is currently not available. The staff member who sends the **first reply** to an unassigned inquiry is automatically assigned to it.

**Q. Why does the assignee show as "Unassigned"?**

A. No staff member has been assigned to the inquiry yet. Once a staff member sends the first reply, that staff member is automatically assigned.

**Q. Can customers see the labels or groups applied to them?**

A. No. **Labels and groups are used for internal consultation management and are not visible to customers.**
