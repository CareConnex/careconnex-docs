### AI Multilingual Consultation

Dr.OZ AI Multilingual Consultation helps staff quickly understand and respond to foreign-language inquiries.

When a customer sends a message in another language, Dr.OZ automatically detects the customer’s language, analyzes the conversation, and generates a suggested reply in that language together with a Korean translation.

#### How to Use AI Multilingual Consultation

① Select the **\[Dr.OZ AI]** tab on the right.

② Under **Suggested Responses**, review the response generated in the customer's language together with the Korean translation.

③ Click the **apply icon (paper plane icon)** on the suggested response you want to use.

* The selected response is automatically inserted into the message input field.
* Review or edit the message if needed, then press **Enter** or click **Send** to send it to the customer.

### 💡 Things to Keep in Mind

* Replies suggested to customers are automatically generated based on the **language used by the customer**.
* Since Dr.OZ AI automatically detects the customer’s language, staff do **not** need to manually choose a translation language for each conversation.
* AI-generated translations and suggested replies are designed to support the consultation process. Always review the reply before sending it to make sure it matches the customer’s inquiry and your intended response.
* Even if **Dr.OZ Settings > Menu Language** is set to **Korean**, suggested replies will still be generated in the **customer’s language** once that language is detected.
