# Change Appointment Status

### Change an Appointment Status

Update an appointment status to keep track of each patient's current appointment progress.

1. Go to the **Schedule** menu.
2. Select the appointment you want to update.
3. Click the **Appointment Status** dropdown at the top of the appointment details window.
4. Select the new status.
5. Click **\[Save]**.

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/image (773).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

**✅ Appointment Statuses**

* **Pending**: The appointment has been created and is waiting to be confirmed.
* **Confirmed**: The appointment has been confirmed.
* **Completed**: The consultation or treatment has been completed.
* **Canceled**: The appointment has been canceled.
* **No-Show**: The patient did not attend the appointment.

***

### Appointment Status Handling

#### General Appointment Status

* A typical appointment progresses through **Pending → Confirmed → Completed**.
* If an appointment remains **Pending** after its scheduled time has passed, the system automatically changes it to **Confirmed**.

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/image (774).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

> 💡 **Appointment reminders are sent only for appointments with the Confirmed status.**

***

#### **No-Show Handling**

* When an appointment is changed to **No-Show**, any deducted ticket session is automatically restored.
* When changing a No-Show appointment to **Completed**, you can choose whether to deduct a ticket session.
* You can review the status change in the **\[History]** tab of the appointment details window.

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/image (777).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

> 💡 A **No-Show** appointment can only be changed to **Completed**.
