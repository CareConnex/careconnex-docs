# Edit an Appointment

### Edit Appointment Details

1. Go to the **Schedule** menu.
2. Click the appointment you want to edit.
3. The appointment details window will open.
4. Click the ✏️ **Edit** icon at the bottom-right.
5. Update the necessary information.
   * Editable fields will become active after you click the **Edit** icon.
6. Click **\[Save]** to apply your changes.

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/image (763).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

**✅ What You Can Edit**

* Appointment status (**Pending, Completed, Cancelled, No-Show**)
* Appointment and time
* Assigned staff member (**Member / Doctor**)
* Memo

***

### Reschedule an Appointment

You can change an appointment’s date or time directly from the Schedule calendar using any of the following three methods.

#### 1. Drag and Drop

Drag the appointment to the desired time slot on the calendar.

* Use this method when changing the appointment time **within the same day**.

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/image (764).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

***

#### 2. Cut and Paste

Use **Cut and Paste** when moving an appointment to a different date or staff member.

1. Right-click the appointment you want to move and select **\[Cut]**.
2. Go to the desired date or staff member’s schedule.
3. Right-click the desired time slot and select **\[Paste]**.

Once the confirmation message appears, the appointment has been successfully moved.

***

#### 3. Change the Date from the Service Appointment Window

Open the appointment details and click the ✏️ **\[Edit]** button. Then select the **calendar icon** next to the appointment date.

* Choose the new date from the calendar.
* If you also need to change the assigned staff member, you can update it in the same appointment window.

### 📌 Notes

* When the **appointment date or time** is changed, an **appointment update notification** is automatically sent with the updated schedule.
* Changing only the assigned staff member, memo, or other information without changing the date or time **does not send another appointment update notification**.
* Appointment reminders are sent according to the configured reminder schedule and are **not resent immediately just because an appointment was edited**.

***

### ❓ Frequently Asked Questions

#### Q. Why wasn’t an appointment update notification sent?

**A.** If an appointment is updated several times within a short period, WhatsApp may detect the repeated messages as duplicate or spam-like activity, which may prevent some notifications from being delivered.

Whenever possible, confirm the appointment details first and make the necessary changes at once.

#### Q. Will the reminder be sent again after I edit an appointment?

**A.** No. Reminders are sent according to the configured reminder conditions and scheduled time, so editing an appointment does not immediately resend the reminder.

If the **appointment date or time** is changed, a separate **appointment update notification** will be sent.
