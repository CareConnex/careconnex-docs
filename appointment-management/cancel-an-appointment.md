# Cancel an Appointment

### Cancel an Appointment

1. Go to the **Schedule** menu and select the appointment you want to cancel.
2. In the appointment details window, select **\[Cancel]** from the **Appointment Status** dropdown.
3. When the **“Are you sure you want to cancel this appointment?”** confirmation window appears, click **\[Confirm]**.

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/image (772).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

4. When the **“Appointment cancelled successfully”** message appears at the bottom of the screen, the cancellation is complete.
5. Check that the cancelled appointment is displayed in **grey** on the calendar.

> **\[Insert screenshot: Appointment before and after cancellation]**

***

### 📌 Notes

* Cancelled appointments are displayed in **grey** on the calendar. Once an appointment has been cancelled, its status cannot be changed back to **Confirmed, No-Show**, or another appointment status.
* When an appointment is cancelled, any **deducted ticket session is automatically restored**. You can check the ticket restoration history in the **\[History]** tab of the appointment details window.
