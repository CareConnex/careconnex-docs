# Create an Appointment

## Create an Appointment from the Schedule

From the **Schedule**, you can create either a **Service Appointment** or a **Consultation Appointment**.

Click an **open time slot** on the calendar, or click **\[+ Appointment]** in the upper-right corner to start creating an appointment.

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/image (757).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>



***

### Service Appointment

Select **Service** when booking a treatment or procedure that uses a ticket purchased by the patient.

① Under the **Service** tab, search for and select the patient by name.\
② Under **Purchased Tickets**, select the ticket to use for this appointment.\
③ Select the service, staff member, date, and time.\
④ Review the appointment details, then click **\[Confirm]** to create the appointment.

> 💡 If the patient does not appear in the search results, click **\[+ Add Patient]** to register them. If the patient does not have an available ticket, click **\[+ Add a ticket]**.

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/image (759).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

> #### 💡 Tips
>
> * If a ticket includes multiple services, select only the service or services to be provided during this appointment.
> * To change the appointment date, click the calendar icon and select a new date.

***

### Consultation Appointment

You can create consultation appointments for either **new** or **returning** patients.

Under the **Consultation** tab, select **New** or **Returning**, then enter the required appointment information.

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/image (761).png" alt="" width="483"><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

#### New Patient

* Enter the patient's **name** and **mobile number**, then set the consultation date and time.
* Select the staff member in charge and click **\[Confirm]** to create the appointment.

#### Returning Patient

* Search for the patient by name and select them from the results.
* If the patient does not appear in the search results, click **\[+ Add Patient]** to register them first.
* Select the staff member in charge and click **\[Confirm]** to create the appointment.

***

### 📌 Notes

* A **Service Appointment** can only be created when the patient has an available ticket.
* Appointment statuses progress in the following order: **\[Pending] → \[Confirmed] → \[Completed]**.
* To share the same ticket with another patient, use the **Additional Patients** section below the Notes field. Sessions used by additional patients will also be deducted from the same ticket.
* Appointments requested through the patient app appear under **\[Pending]**. Click **\[View]** to review the request, then click **\[Confirm]** to finalize the appointment.

### ❓ Frequently Asked Questions

**Q. The \[Confirm] button is disabled.**\
A. Check that all required fields have been completed. You may also be unable to create the appointment if the selected time falls outside the assigned staff member's working hours. Check the staff schedule and try again.

**Q. I can't find the patient in the Patient Appointment window.**\
A. Check the patient's name and search again. If the patient has not been registered yet, click **\[+ Add Patient]** and register the patient before creating the appointment.
