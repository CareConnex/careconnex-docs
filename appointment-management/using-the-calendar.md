### Treatment Assignment

The **Treatment Assignment** dashboard provides an at-a-glance view of treatment assignments and distribution across staff members.

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/image (755).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

#### ✅ Key Features

* **Change the reporting period:** View treatment activity by **Day, Week, or Month**.
* **Review treatment counts:** Check the total number of treatments, including completed and canceled treatments, for the selected period.
* **Compare staff assignments:** Compare the number and distribution of treatments assigned to each staff member.
* **Review treatment distribution:** See which treatments are assigned to each staff member and how many have been assigned.

### ❓ Frequently Asked Questions

**Q. How can I quickly return to today's schedule?**\
A. Click **\[Today]** at the top of the calendar.

**Q. How can I view appointments for a specific staff member?**\
A. Use **\[Filter]** in the upper-right corner and select the staff member you want to view.

**Q. How can I change an appointment time?**\
A. Drag the appointment to a different time slot, or update the time directly from the appointment details.

**Q. Where can I set staff leave or unavailable hours?**\
A. Go to **\[More] > \[Schedule Block]** in the upper-right corner.

**Q. Why are appointments displayed in different colors?**\
A. Appointment colors are based on the color assigned to each service. You can update service colors from the **Services** menu.
